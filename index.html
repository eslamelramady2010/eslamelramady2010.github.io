Game rentals & eventsB2C + B2BBuilt from zero

Adventure World (عالم المغامرة) rents bouncy castles, electric trains, hospitality carts and full event setups across Riyadh. When I joined it had no store and no social presence. I built both and made them the company's main booking channel.

up to 20×ROAS on the strongest campaigns

  • Bouncy castles & inflatables

Each listing built for search and checkout: categories, SEO copy, product photos and Tabby / Tamara instalments.

Sales by period · approx. SAR

Year 1 was a short setup period with no campaigns. In Year 2 monthly sales climbed from about SAR 30K to 50K to 70K, then passed SAR 100K. The best month reached about SAR 500K.

What I executed

Hands-on, end to end

E-commerce store

  • Built the online store from scratch: theme, page structure and category tree.
  • Wrote and published every product listing, with on-page SEO for titles, descriptions and categories.
  • Photographed the products and designed all store banners and graphics.
  • Integrated payment gateways plus Tabby and Tamara so large event bookings could be paid in instalments.

Brand & social

  • Created the visual identity and the content plan used across all channels.
  • Got the Instagram account verified.
  • Designed every highlight: customer photos, certificates, contests, new games, booths, official events and VIP.
  • Ran contests to grow the audience and collect customer content.

Paid media

  • Planned and managed a budget of about SAR 15K a month across four platforms.
  • Ran Google Search as the main channel for high-intent rental searches.
  • Ran Snapchat and Instagram for reach and seasonal demand; added TikTok in the final period.
  • Optimized for bookings rather than clicks, which the SAR 3,000+ order value made highly profitable.

Growth & reporting

  • Took monthly sales from about SAR 30K to over SAR 100K within Year 2.
  • Scaled into event seasons, with single orders reaching SAR 75,000.
  • Tracked sales and ROAS by channel to decide where the next riyal went.

Case 03 · Jordan → KSA · Apr 2026 – now

House of CareA 150+ product Shopify store, built for launch and for the Saudi market

Healthy food DTCShopifyCurrently in launch

House of Care is a Jordanian healthy-food brand with six years of history that sells online only. I own the online store and its launch: building the Shopify store, getting the product data right, setting up WhatsApp ordering and planning the first ad campaigns. Next comes Saudi Arabia, added as a second market on the same store.

150+products listed and audited
18collections structured for browsing and SEO
14nutrition fields per product, stored as metafields
2markets: Jordan live, KSA via Shopify Markets

What I executed

Store, data, conversion, launch

Store build

  • Built houseofcarejo.com on Shopify: catalogue, 18 collections, navigation and policy pages.
  • Set up shipping rules and offers, including free delivery over 30 JOD.
  • Wrote custom Liquid sections for the product page: an Arabic right-to-left Nutrition Facts table and a NutriScore badge.

Product data & quality

  • Stored 14 nutrition fields for each product as metafields, so the product pages fill themselves.
  • Ran a full pre-launch audit and fixed the blockers: zero prices, leftover demo content, broken links and policy gaps.
  • Checked every live product page on desktop and mobile after each fix.

Conversion & messaging

  • Set up a WhatsApp chatbot that answers Click-to-WhatsApp ad leads and sends order updates.
  • Planned a second WhatsApp number for broadcast campaigns to customer lists.

Launch & growth

  • Planned the first Meta campaigns: Sales objective with broad targeting, then retargeting from week two.
  • Produced pre-launch content, including AI-generated product videos with Arabic voiceover.
  • Prepared the Saudi entry: market research, keyword research and Shopify Markets on the existing store.

The store has just launched, so there are no sales figures to show yet. This case is about the build.
